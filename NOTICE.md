## Statement of modification

Provided under Apache-2.0 §4(b) and GPL-3.0 §5(a). Sora Launcher is **not**
affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Lawnchair project or its
contributors. Significant changes relative to upstream Lawnchair:

- Application identity changed to `com.rennsproject.soralauncher`
  (`.play` and `.nightly` variants for the respective distribution channels).
- Product name changed from "Lawnchair" to "Sora Launcher" throughout the
  user-facing interface and all bundled translations.
- Launcher iconography replaced with original Sora Launcher artwork.
- Ongoing: user interface restyled away from Google Material Launcher
  conventions toward an OEM-style design language.
