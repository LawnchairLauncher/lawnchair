# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-14

Mica Launcher does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to
the developers or any third party. Mica Launcher does not include
analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.

## Data stored on your device

Mica Launcher stores its settings, layout, and cache (such as app icons
and widget state) locally on your device, using Android's standard app
storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you
uninstall the app.

## Permissions

Mica Launcher requests Android permissions (e.g. wallpaper, widgets,
notifications, package queries) solely to provide launcher functionality
on-device. None of the data accessed through these permissions is sent
off the device by Mica Launcher.

## Third-party services

Some optional features (for example, search providers reachable from
the search bar) send your query directly to that third-party service
when you use them, subject to that service's own privacy policy. Mica
Launcher itself does not relay, log, or store these queries.

## Network access

Mica Launcher may make network requests you initiate directly, such as
checking for app updates on GitHub or loading content you choose to
open. Mica Launcher does not run background network calls to collect
data about you.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Changes will be posted
in this file and reflected in the app's release notes.
